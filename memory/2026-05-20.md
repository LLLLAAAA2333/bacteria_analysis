Built
`.worktrees/chemical_survey/exploratory/chemical_survey/explain_neural_pcs_with_chemical_features.py`
to align active-scaled signed-peak neural PC1-PC3 with chemical abundance,
chemical PCs, and taxonomy groups. Chemical abundance used
`data/data_fc_missingto1_filtered.xlsx`, transformed as log2 fold-change and
z-scored within the 52 neural-overlap samples. Taxonomy metadata came from
`data/metabolism_raw_data.xlsx`.

Outputs were written to
`.worktrees/chemical_survey/results/chemical_survey/neural_pc_chemical_feature_alignment/`,
including `chemical_abundance_vs_neural_pc_correlations.csv`,
`chemical_pc_vs_neural_pc_correlations.csv`,
`taxonomy_group_vs_neural_pc_correlations.csv`, `top_alignment_summary.csv`,
scatter plots, top-alignment bar plots, and a chemical-PC heatmap.

Initial readout: NeuralPC1 aligns most strongly with Arginine abundance
(`r=-0.679`, by-PC q `1.0e-5`, global q `3.0e-5`) and Trimethyllysine
(`r=-0.548`, global q `0.0125`). Chemical PCs are weak overall; best absolute
alignment is ChemPC7 with NeuralPC3 (`r=-0.362`, global q `0.222`). Class-level
candidates include Indoles and derivatives for NeuralPC2 (`r=0.418`, by-level
q `0.0405`, global q `0.504`) and Hydroxy acids and derivatives for NeuralPC3
(`r=-0.401`, by-level q `0.0647`, global q `0.504`). Treat taxonomy candidates
as exploratory until validated.

Added
`.worktrees/chemical_survey/exploratory/chemical_survey/plot_neural_pc_chemical_scatter_diagnostics.py`
to plot the top single-metabolite and small class-axis alignments as scatter
diagnostics. Outputs were written under
`.worktrees/chemical_survey/results/chemical_survey/neural_pc_chemical_feature_alignment/scatter_diagnostics/`,
including `top_single_metabolite_scatter_diagnostics.png`,
`top_small_class_axis_scatter_diagnostics.png`,
`scatter_leave_one_out_r_ranges.png`, and
`scatter_leave_one_out_diagnostics.csv`.

Leave-one-out checks suggest the strongest single-metabolite signals are not
single-sample artifacts: Arginine versus NeuralPC1 stays between `r=-0.774` and
`r=-0.569` after removing any one sample; O-Succinyhomoserine versus NeuralPC2
stays between `r=-0.480` and `r=-0.425`; 4-Trimethylammoniobutanoic acid versus
NeuralPC3 stays between `r=0.408` and `r=0.533`. Some class-axis signals are
less robust: Indoles versus NeuralPC2 is stable (`r=0.393` to `0.445`), while
Hydroxy acids versus NeuralPC3 can drop to `r=-0.243` after removing A151.
