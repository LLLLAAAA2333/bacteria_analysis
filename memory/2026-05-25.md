Ran the same direct-neuron marker screen for ASH and AWA under
`results/chemical_survey/ash_awa_marker_screen/`. Both used
`scaled_signed_peak > Q3 + 1.5*IQR` as the primary high-response rule, raw
chemistry filtered by QCRSD <= 0.2 and <=30% missing within the 52 neural
samples, and filtered fold-change support from `data_fc_missingto1_filtered`.

ASH primary-high samples were A137, A151, A179, A181, and A187. No raw
continuous chemical correlation passed FDR < 0.05; fold-change had p-Octopamine
as a weak continuous lead. ASH high-response group differences were strongest
for depleted hydroxy/organic acids and selected amino acids, plus enriched
10-Hydroxydecanoate, trans-Ferulic acid, Epinephrine, Nicotinic acid, and
Phenylalanine.

AWA primary-high samples were A029, A178, A179, A187, and A200. Dimethylamine
was the only raw continuous FDR < 0.05 lead and was depleted in AWA-high
samples. AWA high-response group differences were strongest for depleted
Carnosine, Pipecolic acid, Histidine, Ophthalmic acid, and related amino-acid
signals, with enriched Ureidopropionic acid, Rhamnose, N-Acetylmethionine, and
long-chain fatty-acid leads.
