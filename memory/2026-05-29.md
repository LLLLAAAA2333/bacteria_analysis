## 20:44 | Ran 86bac neural-shape versus chemical-PCA RSA

- kind: exploratory-analysis
- tags: [86bac, neural-rdm, chemical-rdm, rsa, active-scaling, pca]
- topic: neural-representation
- source: user request + local analysis
- promotion_hint: candidate
- related_state: [state/heartbeat-state.json]
- related_memory: [MEM-20260529-001]

Added and ran `exploratory/compute_86bac_shape_pca_rsa.py`. Outputs were written
under `results/86bac_shape_pca_rsa/`. The analysis used 86 sample-level neural
prototypes from `data/86bac.parquet`, L/R merge, active-scaled flattened traces
over window 10:30, and neural shape distance `1 - corr`. Chemical side used
`data/data_fc_missingto1.xlsx`, retained 256 features with QCRSD <= 0.2 and raw
missing rate <= 0.5, then log2 fold-change, per-feature z-score, first 10 PCs,
and Euclidean distance in PC space. The first 10 chemical PCs explained 68.47%
of variance. RSA over 86 shared samples and 3655 pairs gave Spearman 0.153067,
Pearson 0.193106, and label-shuffle p(greater) 0.000400 with 5000
permutations. A no-ASG sanity check gave Spearman 0.146590, so the result does
not depend strongly on ASG's fallback active scale.
