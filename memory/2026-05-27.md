## 18:33 | Project scientific framing

- kind: decision

- topic: project-direction
- source: user
- promotion_hint: promoted
- related_state: [state/heartbeat-state.json]
- related_memory: [MEM-20260527-001]

User framed the central project question as what C. elegans neural responses
represent and how to interpret them, rather than as an engineering problem of
proving response stability. The session focus is to evaluate this framing, not
to propose new analysis steps. User also emphasized that neural activity is
multidimensional and should not be reduced too casually to peaks or flattened
traces, but chemical/metabolic space is the harder high-dimensional problem:
metabolites have precursor/product and pathway relationships, composition is
complex, and naive full-space Euclidean PCA or RDM distances can be misleading.
