## MEM-20260515-002 Category concentration PCA alignment

Generated a local, gitignored PCA-concentration alignment package for the RSA
shared-structure chemical categories at
`results/202604_without_20260331/taxonomy_category_pca_concentration_alignment`.

The reusable script is
`data/local_taxonomy_category_performance_review/run_category_concentration_pca_alignment.py`.
It uses the current neural PCA context: all neurons included, absolute-active
scaled signed-peak features, PCA window `10:30`. Chemical values are
`log1p(raw concentration)` from `data/metabolism_raw_data.xlsx`; category
members are filtered with `QCRSD <= 0.2` to match the taxonomy RSA review.

Category-aggregate correlations with PC axes:

- `Class::Pyridines and derivatives` aligns most with `PC2`: `r = 0.406`,
  `p = 0.0028`.
- `Class::Phenylpropanoic acids` aligns negatively with `PC2`: `r = -0.341`,
  `p = 0.0134`.
- `SubClass::Pyridinecarboxylic acids and derivatives` has weaker positive
  `PC2` alignment: `r = 0.172`.
- `Class::Purine nucleosides` is weak at the aggregate level, with its largest
  correlation on `PC3`: `r = 0.117`.
- `Class::Indoles and derivatives` is also weak as an aggregate, but individual
  metabolites show stronger associations, especially
  `5-Hydroxyindole-3-acetic Acid` with `PC3`: `r = 0.609`, `p = 4.96e-05`.

## MEM-20260515-003 Category chemical PCA alignment

Generated a local, gitignored category-specific chemical PCA alignment package at
`results/202604_without_20260331/taxonomy_category_chemical_pca_alignment`.

The reusable script is
`data/local_taxonomy_category_performance_review/run_category_chemical_pca_alignment.py`.
It keeps the same neural PCA context as the sensitivity analysis: all neurons
included, absolute-active scaled signed-peak features, PCA window `10:30`.
Within each selected chemical category, QC20 metabolites are transformed as
`log1p(raw concentration)`, median-imputed, z-scored, and reduced with up to
three chemical PCs. Chemical PC signs are oriented post hoc so the strongest
neural-PC correlation is positive.

Key exploratory alignments:

- `Class::Diazines cPC2` aligns with neural `PC1`: `r = 0.432`,
  `p = 0.00138`. This was stronger than the diazine category mean, but diazines
  were not a stable RSA headline class, so treat it as diagnostic.
- `Class::Indoles and derivatives cPC2` aligns with neural `PC2`:
  `r = 0.304`, `p = 0.0286`.
- `Class::Organooxygen compounds cPC3` and
  `SuperClass::Organic oxygen compounds cPC3` both align with neural `PC1`:
  `r = 0.299`, `p = 0.0312`; these two sets are identical after QC20 filtering
  in this dataset.
- `Class::Phenylpropanoic acids cPC3` has borderline neural `PC1` alignment:
  `r = 0.274`, `p = 0.0496`.
- `Class::Purine nucleosides` remains weak in direct chemical-PC alignment
  despite being the strongest RSA class, suggesting its RSA signal is not a
  simple one-dimensional concentration gradient in neural PCA space.
- `Class::Pyridines and derivatives` was stronger as a category-mean
  concentration signal than as an internal chemical-PC signal.
