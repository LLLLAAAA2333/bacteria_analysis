# Poster figure

## Short caption for poster layout

**Local chemical states and neural response patterns.** A 14-annotation chemical state accompanies the relative ADF−ASH response in 29 *Bacteroides* strains (A); a 21-annotation state accompanies a fitted 13-neuron direction in 11 *Bifidobacterium* strains (B). Points are individual strains; lines are descriptive fits. Both relationships were selected within these data, and B is a supervised projection. C and D show observed mean responses in chemical-score rank thirds, relative to each genus mean. All 13 neurons share one color scale; each strain's coefficients are normalized by its complete 13-neuron norm. Group sizes are shown, and individual overlap remains visible above. The two genera have different chemical states and neural readouts. These exploratory relationships provide local chemical context for neural response patterns without identifying causal compounds.

## Expanded caption and methods

**Local chemical states and neural response patterns.** Forty strains from two well-sampled genera are shown, retaining all strains in each genus. **A, C:** In *Bacteroides* (29 strains), the previously selected 14-annotation chemical state accompanies a shift in the fixed relative ADF−ASH contrast. **B, D:** In *Bifidobacterium* (11 strains), a 21-annotation chemical state accompanies a broader neural pattern, most visibly involving AWA, AWCOFF, ADF and ASH. Each point represents one strain. The ordinate in B projects the observed 13-coordinate response onto a direction fitted from all 11 displayed strains; it is a descriptive, supervised projection. The two columns use different chemical states and neural readouts, not a common axis. Lines are full-data descriptive fits.

In C and D, every cell is an **observed** group mean minus that neuron's mean across the genus. All 13 rows and both genera share the same color scale; neurons are not individually standardized. Low, middle and high groups are determined only by rank on the selected chemical score (10/10/9 and 4/4/3 strains); their equal widths do not denote equal chemical intervals. Point colors use these same groups. Selection of each chemical state used neural responses, so the grouped patterns and scatterplots describe selected exploratory relationships. Individual chemical annotations and all strain-level neural values are provided in the companion figures.

Chemical scores average standardized log2 report concentrations with equal weights across Mass-column families and then within each family. Bacteroides includes indole-related annotations, phenols/amines, acetylated/formylated amino-acid derivatives and nucleotides; Bifidobacterium includes organic acids, amino acids, amines and vitamins. These are mixed co-varying annotations, not established pathways or identified causal molecules. Neural values are signed template coefficients normalized by each strain's full 13-neuron norm. A positive deviation means a higher relative coefficient, not necessarily excitation. Chemical measurements came from separately cultured material rather than quantified neural-stimulation aliquots. The figure describes local chemical context for the neural representation, not a universal or mechanistic mapping.

## Supporting individual figures

Each column represents one strain, ordered by the exact chemical score used in the poster. The top trace displays this score. Chemical rows show within-genus standardized log2 concentrations for every selected annotation; neural rows show all observed unit coefficients minus the corresponding genus mean. The same strain order is used in all three panels. Vertical dividers mark the poster's rank thirds. Every strain is retained, including Bacteroides source-taxonomy flags recorded in the data table. Color scales in these individual figures cover their entire values and differ from the poster's group-mean scale. Parentheses in displayed annotation names are typographically normalized; exact report names remain in the tables.

## Selection and model provenance

The new complete-vector procedure was evaluated in both genera with at least 10 strains. Its Bacteroides winner was a different three-annotation state and failed the internal leaveout checks (−4.8% strain, −15.7% species). It is retained in the analysis results but does not replace the previously supported Bacteroides contrast example in the poster. The new Bifidobacterium state is selected from 12 candidates using total 13-coordinate training SSE; its exact 21-member list varies across folds (median Jaccard overlap approximately 0.77). The leaveout checks evaluate the selection procedure, not independent confirmation of the final 21-member list. Complete-vector benefit is concentrated chiefly in AWCOFF and AWA, and does not imply association in every neuron.
