# Figure captions

## 01_animal_repeat_vs_between

**Repeat scatter is smaller than between-strain differences, with appreciable residual variation.** Each point is one of 100 saved, overlapping date-balanced whole-animal A/B splits. Both halves use the same observed date×neuron support (at least two animals per half), averaged equally over shared dates for each neuron. All panels and representations use the same jointly complete, nonzero 13-neuron strain set within a split (13–21 strains; median 20). Same-strain RMS is √meanᵢ‖Aᵢ−Bᵢ‖²; different-strain RMS uses all ordered i≠j pairs. The horizontal line shows the median; the vertical line shows 5th–95th split percentiles, not a confidence interval. PC1/PC2 positions use the frozen full-29 mean and loadings. SNR-gated and pre-gate values share frozen full-data templates and observation support. RMS scales differ across panels. The shared-date subset is not the original all-date estimand; neither these splits nor the templates provide end-to-end independent validation.

## 02_cross_date_pairs

**The six cross-date pairs show different repeat behavior for ADF−ASH and AWB.** Left: one point per strain, with the earlier recording on x and the later recording on y; the dashed line denotes equal values. These are SNR-gated full-13 unit coordinates. Asterisks preserve `source_note_check` metadata for A025 and A044, without asserting a confirmed taxonomy error. Full species labels and both dates are in README.md and the saved tables. Right: the RMS of six later-minus-earlier changes is compared with the RMS of all 15 unique differences between the six two-date mean profiles. The two references have different measurement precision. The small, nonrandom subset shares calendar dates and animals; recording on different dates does not establish independent culture replication. Ratios are descriptive references, not reliability coefficients. The pre-gate comparison uses the same fixed templates.

## 03_half_planes_and_coverage

**Animal halves do not consistently recover both plane directions, and complete-profile support excludes some strains.** Left: the two principal angles between top-two loading subspaces fitted separately to the two halves, using the same jointly complete strains within each split, centering without coordinate-wise SD scaling. Each point is a saved split; median and 5th–95th split percentiles are shown. These are half-sample plane estimates, not fixed-plane position differences or full-data delete-strain stability. Right: number of splits in which each of the original 29 strains has complete shared observation support and nonzero gated/pre-gate coefficient norms. Eight strains have no eligible complete-profile split; zero coverage is not evidence of low reliability. Splits overlap and are not independent experiments.
