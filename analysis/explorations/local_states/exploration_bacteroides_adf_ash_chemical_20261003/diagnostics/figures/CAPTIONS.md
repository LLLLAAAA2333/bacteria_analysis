# Figure captions

## 01_chemical_relationship_and_heldout

**A selected chemical state is associated with the fixed ADF−ASH contrast, with substantial individual scatter.** Left: all 29 Bacteroides strains on the full-cohort selected chemical state (14 annotations, 13 Mass-column families), with the saved OLS fit. ADF−ASH is the difference between two coordinates after L2 normalization of the complete 13-neuron coefficient vector. Three strain labels mark the largest x-only OLS leverage values; no strains are removed. Middle and right: each strain's held-out observation and prediction, after all chemical fitting and primary-axis selection were repeated inside its training fold; dashed lines denote equality. Pooled squared prediction error is 23.1% lower than the training-fold mean baseline for leave-one-strain-out and 20.0% lower for leave-one-recorded-species-out. Colors represent complete original recording-date sets; outlined points preserve taxonomy-note flags without asserting confirmed errors. Full species/date/strain mapping is shown in Figure 03. The target was prioritized using earlier analyses of this cohort, and temporal neural templates remain frozen from the original atlas. These are exploratory, representation-conditional checks, not a wholly independent discovery or a causal dose response.

## 02_adf_ash_thirds_and_all13_effects

**The fitted direction involves ADF and ASH, but low and middle chemical thirds overlap.** Top: all 29 strains and the saved same-state linear fits for ADF and ASH separately, using identical unit-coordinate y-axis limits. Bottom left: individual primary contrast values in chemical-score rank thirds of 10, 10 and 9 strains, assigned after score then strain-ID ordering. Bin cutpoints use score ranks only, conditional on a chemical axis already selected using the full-cohort primary response; the thirds are same-data descriptions, not independent validation. Horizontal bars are medians; horizontal jitter is deterministic and conveys no additional variable. Bottom right: fitted changes of all 13 unit coordinates over the same observed chemical-score IQR (0.944); these are full-data descriptive slopes without independent selection or uncertainty intervals. Colors and outlines in the scatter panels follow Figure 01. Unit coordinates have a shared 13-neuron denominator; the same-axis unnormalized ADF/ASH slopes are also saved as descriptive normalization checks. Opposing cross-strain slopes do not establish opposing within-strain responses to an intervention.

## 03_chemical_ordered_all13_context

**All 29 strains retain complete neural and recording context in chemical-score order.** Rows are ordered only by the full-data selected chemical score, then strain ID; horizontal separators mark the fixed 10/10/9 rank thirds. The left bar shows the score using the same original-date-set colors as Figures 01–02. The heatmap retains all 13 original signed unit coordinates, without coordinate-wise SD scaling, on one symmetric color scale. Direct labels show recorded species and complete date sets (YYYYMMDD; semicolons preserve multiple dates). Asterisks retain the six taxonomy-note flags; no flagged or repeat-coverage-limited strain is excluded. Heatmap color represents a relative template coefficient, not excitation/inhibition, a proportion, or raw calcium fluorescence.
