# Step 3: Independent genus chemical patterns

Protocol fixed before calculation of clustering or pattern results (2026-10-03).

Scope: describe genus-associated chemical combinations independently of Step 4. No neural values, neural ordering, prediction models, cross-modal correspondence, or mechanistic/pathway attribution are used. All 13 genera with at least two matched strains are included (90 strains); the other 16 singleton genera are recorded as coverage only. Source strain IDs and metabolite IDs are aligned explicitly.

Inputs: `fresh_chemical_log2.csv`, `fresh_feature_metadata.csv`, and `sample_context.csv` under `reports/exploration_chemical_pattern_direct_report_20261003/tables`. The 162 report annotations have complete positive concentrations and passed the source QC rule; values are log2(c / 1 ng/mL), without +1 or new imputation. The annotations need not be chemically independent and the selected panel is not a complete metabolome.

## Fixed representation

For metabolite f and genus g, C[g,f] is the arithmetic mean of log2 concentration across strains in g. R[f] is the arithmetic mean of the 13 C[g,f] values, giving every genus equal reference weight. S[f] is their sample SD (ddof=1). Z[g,f]=(C[g,f]-R[f])/S[f]. Individual strain values use the identical R and S. Both the original log2 values and their log2 deviations from R are retained; Z is used only to compare patterns, not concentration or total abundance. Features with S <= 1e-12 log2 units (numerical zero), if encountered, will be excluded from standardized clustering and reported rather than silently perturbed. All other features are retained without searching an amplitude cutoff; small nonzero between-genus SD can magnify small original log2 differences, so S and raw log2 ranges are saved and large Z is not called large absolute concentration change.

## Fixed grouping and ordering

Metabolite modules use Pearson correlation of the 13 genus-center profiles, average linkage on distance 1-r, and a single distance cut of 0.5. Clusters of at least 3 report annotations spanning at least 3 distinct Mass-column families form modules; all other clusters remain ungrouped. Family is the source metadata `family` identifier. No threshold or alternative grouping is searched. Modules are descriptive between-genus co-variation groups; they do not establish within-genus co-regulation or pathways. All 162 annotations remain in the full tables and supporting figure. IDs C01, C02, ... are assigned by decreasing module size, breaking ties by alphabetical first member.

Each strain's module score is the mean of member Z values within each Mass-column family, followed by the equal-weight mean across families; genus module scores are the mean across that genus's strains. Genus display order uses average linkage on Euclidean distance of all retained 162-feature Z center vectors. Module display order uses average linkage on distance 1-r of the genus module-score profiles. Feature display order follows the original chemical linkage. All orders and members are exported; no order is imported from other analyses.

## Dispersion, consistency, and sensitivity

For every genus-feature and genus-module cell save n, mean, SD, minimum, quartiles, maximum, and counts above/below the equal-genus reference. For modules also save the fraction of strains on the genus center's side of zero, both unweighted and family-weighted fractions of member genus centers on that side, and the range of leave-one-strain-out genus centers with module membership/scales fixed while recomputing the omitted genus center and the equal-genus reference. Removing one strain tests conditional sensitivity of the observed center, not independent validation. A genus participates in its own equal-genus reference; recomputing that reference does not remove this circularity, and modules/scales were learned from the full chemical data. A descriptive `direction_supported` flag requires at least 75% of strains and a family-weighted fraction of at least 75% of member genus centers on the center's side, with every leave-one-strain-out center retaining that direction. Direction uses numerical tolerance 1e-12 in score/Z units. A zero genus center has no direction and is never flagged; zero strain/member values stay in the full n/member denominator and count as neither direction. The strain fraction denominator is all observed strains in that genus. Direction refers only to the equal-genus reference, which includes the target genus. This flag is not a significance test and is not used to select which modules or genera are plotted. Stronger descriptive examples use absolute genus module score >=0.5 in addition to this predeclared flag. All nonconforming cells remain displayed.

For every module save member-pair correlation summaries across genus centers, across all 90 individual strains with observation weight 1/(13*n_g), and across strain residuals after subtracting that strain's genus mean, using the same weights. The last summary distinguishes between-genus co-variation from residual within-genus co-variation; these correlations give equal total weight to each genus and are diagnostics, not a separate independent validation. Save both all member pairs and cross-family member pairs. Small-sample genera and nested/shared sample dependence prevent interpreting pair counts as replication.

## Outputs and limits

Main figure: all chemical module-by-genus means, English labels, fixed symmetric color scale; no molecule singled out as the main story. Supporting figures retain per-strain module scores, direction fractions, and all 162 features. No inferential p-values, confident biological labels, or cross-modal comparison. Uneven genus sample sizes, small n, culture/measurement variation, shared selection data, possible taxonomic substructure, incomplete chemical panel, correlated/redundant report annotations, and no independent chemical culture replication limit claims to this observed panel.

Code consists of small functions and a callable `run_analysis(repo_root)` entry point suitable for an existing Notebook, without creating or executing a Notebook. Raw data and earlier outputs are not modified. Input SHA256 hashes, parameters, package versions, exact members/orders, reproducible tables, and independent numeric verification are saved.

Display-only clarification after calculation: C01–C15 are new chemical-genus groups in this analysis, renamed from draft M IDs to avoid confusion with historical score/module names. Members and numbers are unchanged. Representative annotation names are the top three member correlations with the module genus profile (descending, ties alphabetical); this is only a label/lookup rule and does not select module results or imply biological functions.
