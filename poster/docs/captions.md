## Figure 1. Response patterns across bacterial stimuli

Reconstructed calcium-response profiles for 106 bacterial stimuli across 13 neuron classes. Each neuron is represented by the first five 5-s bins of a shared 0–40-s temporal template multiplied by a signed condition-level amplitude, followed by equal weighting of available condition blocks within a strain. The individual-SNR threshold is 0.5, with the saved minimum-animal rule. Dashed lines mark stimulus offset at 10 s. Colors show reconstructed ΔF/F₀; values outside the displayed range are saturated, and missing values are gray. Strain order follows the previously saved hierarchy of unfiltered full response profiles. Screened zeros are analysis outcomes, not evidence of physiological absence.

**Method strip.** A saved A300–AWCON mean response illustrates 5-s binning and its approximation by a shared RMS-normalized template and a signed amplitude. This previously selected strong-response example illustrates the calculation rather than a typical response.

## Figure 2. Within-strain repeatability of neural patterns

Split-half cosine similarities for the saved 13-neuron representation. The within-strain distribution contains 106 strain-level mean similarities; the between-strain distribution contains 5,396 finite unique strain-pair means, with 169 undefined pairs omitted. Mean similarities are 0.818 and 0.443, respectively. Densities use identical bins and are normalized within each group. Split assignments overlap across the 100 repeats, and pairs share strains; the plotted values are descriptive distributions rather than independent replicates. Support counts, matched representation controls and held-out prediction checks are provided in the accompanying notebook.

## Figure 3. Global chemical and neural distances

Neural and chemical distance matrices for the same 106 strains, shown in the same saved response-profile order. Neural distance is one minus cosine similarity between the 13-neuron signed-amplitude vectors. Chemical distance is the RMS difference across 380 log₂ fold-change annotations, prepared by filling missing concentrations with zero, adding one, and applying the original sample-specific reference normalization. The hexagonal-bin panel summarizes counts among all 5,565 unique matched strain pairs (gridsize = 27, RdBu_r). Descriptive correspondence is Pearson r = 0.262 and Spearman ρ = 0.213. The two matrices have different units and separate scales; the neural color scale spans 0–2. Pairs share strains and are not independent replicates, so ordinary pairwise-correlation p values are not reported.

**Alternative concentration representation (supporting).** The same raw chemical measurements also yield 162 annotations after retaining complete, positive concentrations with QC RSD ≤ 0.30 and taking log₂ concentration. This alternative gives Pearson r = 0.175 and Spearman ρ = 0.185 across the same 5,565 pairs. Its neural heatmap uses 0–1.2, saturating 72 pairs (1.3%) above 1.2; the upper colorbar extension indicates saturation, and calculations retain all original distances. The pair panel uses a Blues count hexbin (gridsize = 25). This representation remains the chemical input for Figure 4. The saved full-106 HMDS comparison shares the 380-annotation chemical definition of the main Figure 3 and is retained as supporting exploration.

## Figure 4. Local chemical states accompany neural configurations

Local chemical–neural relationships in Bacteroides (29 strains) and Bifidobacterium (11 strains). Chemical-state scores average within-genus standardized log₂ concentrations with equal weight per Mass-column family and equal weight among annotations within a family. The Bacteroides state contains 14 annotations selected using the previously fixed ADF−ASH contrast. The Bifidobacterium state contains 21 annotations selected to minimize training error across all 13 neural coordinates; its displayed neural projection is fitted on the same data and is descriptive. Lower panels show observed 13-neuron mean profiles after subtracting the genus mean, in low, middle and high score rank groups (10/10/9 and 4/4/3 strains, respectively), using a shared color scale. Full vectors are displayed without implying that every neuron is associated with the state. Selection-aware internal leave-out results and the unsuccessful Bacteroides all-coordinate extension are retained in the notebook. These local associations do not establish a universal mapping or a causal mechanism.
